# Shortly production work

For video, storyboard, media generation, or CapCut production work, consult the
applicable sections of `docs/shorts-production-style-guide.md`. Latest dated user
decisions override older production notes. Do not apply video-editing preferences
to unrelated software tasks.

- Start revisions from the latest saved native CapCut draft, not an earlier build
  script. Back up first; preserve edits outside the requested scope and the same
  project identity. Never overwrite an open draft.
- For real products, verify era/version and reference appearance before selecting
  or generating assets. Physical-design explanations need actual product details,
  not generic containers. Match body, proportions, nozzle/cap, label and logo.
- For each cut record what may appear now and what must wait. Read surrounding
  narration to resolve context, but do not illustrate later colors/products early.
  A noun match is insufficient when the key modifier or action is absent.
- Inspect actual generated pixels against the reference and the cut's narration
  before publishing. Prompt checks and text-only model reviews are not pixel QA.
- Verify timeline coverage, sentence spacing, captions, media framing and ending
  hold. Distinguish code-rendered review copies from native CapCut exports.
- Record sources and actual reuse-license status; AI editing does not establish
  permission. Do not label an unverified image CC0/CC BY.
- Do not automatically retry paid media requests. Preserve failed-request details
  and ask before a further charge when required by the user's production budget.
- Before paid synthesis or generation, apply the editorial preflight and the
  2026-10-25 whole-video review in the style guide. Retain comprehension
  prerequisites; assess the combined narration/visual first impression for
  advertorial cues and identify why viewers should continue after the opening.
  Different positive product facts are not automatically distinct story rewards.
  Review the whole sequence again in the actual available render, not just each
  caption/image match. Record specific moments, findings and unverified checks;
  technical checks and blanket review booleans are not editorial approval.
- Apply the 2026-10-26 curiosity-and-reward review: design the title and first
  visual as a pair, state the concrete question they create together, and identify
  fresh viewing rewards across the body. Different images or new nouns alone are
  not new entertainment. Do not restrict this principle to AI criticism, invent
  conflict, or treat one video's reported performance as causal proof.
- Apply the 2026-10-27 flexible-production and preservation rules: prioritize
  promising subjects, usable visual evidence and story progression over extra
  explanation. These are evaluation priorities, not a mandatory narrative order,
  length or negative-topic formula. Preserve already-effective scripts and manual
  edits; no change needed is a valid review outcome. Explain the specific problem,
  expected benefit and potential loss before a discretionary rewrite. Diagnose
  opening choice separately from body retention; aggregate metrics do not prove
  an edit will improve performance or authorize revising existing projects.
- Main subject objects, including unbranded food/ingredients, require real-photo
  references actually supplied to generation and specific pixel comparisons.
  Reject identity/proportion/structure mismatches; use the real asset when faithful
  recreation cannot be achieved. A realistic-looking substitute is not sufficient.
